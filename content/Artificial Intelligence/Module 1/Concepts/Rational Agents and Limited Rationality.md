# Rational Agents and Limited Rationality
_Source slides: 8-10_

## Rational Agent
A rational agent perceives and acts to achieve the best outcome, or the best expected outcome under uncertainty. A performance measure defines what “doing the right thing” means.

## Expected Utility
The lecture combines belief and preference mathematically. See [[Ch01 Mathematics#Expected-Utility Action Rule]].

## Limited Rationality
Perfect rationality assumes unlimited computation. Real agents have finite time, memory, data, and model accuracy. Limited rationality asks what is appropriate given those constraints.

> [!important]
> Approximation is not a defect; it is often the only rational thing to do.

## Metalevel Reasoning
A computation should be selected only if its expected value exceeds its cost. See [[Ch01 Mathematics#Metalevel Computation Rule]].

## Exam-Level Understanding
Be able to distinguish perfect vs limited rationality, action utility vs computation value, and exact optimality vs rational approximation.
