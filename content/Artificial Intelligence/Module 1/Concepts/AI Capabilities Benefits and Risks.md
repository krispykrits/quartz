# AI Capabilities, Benefits, and Risks
_Source slides: 37-39_

## Capabilities
Robotic vehicles/drones, locomotion/manipulation, planning/scheduling, translation/speech, recommendation/spam filtering, games, vision, medical imaging, climate science, and scientific discovery.

> [!warning]
> Benchmark performance is not the same as robust, transparent, beneficial behavior in open-ended environments.

## Benefits
- accelerate science and medicine,
- reduce dangerous/repetitive labor,
- improve logistics, translation, accessibility, education,
- help address climate/resource problems.

## Risks
- autonomous weapons,
- mass surveillance and persuasion,
- biased decision making,
- labor disruption and inequality,
- safety-critical failures,
- cybersecurity amplification.

## Governance
Slide 38 summarizes: `AI capability → governance standards → social outcome`.

## Long-Term Alignment
Slide 39 adds AGI/superintelligence, the control problem, King Midas failures, and preference uncertainty.
