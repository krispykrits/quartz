## Major Connections
- **Logic → probability → decision:** certainty, uncertain belief, then action.
- **Search → explosion → knowledge/learning:** scale limits motivate structure.
- **Fixed objective → specification error → alignment:** stronger optimization can amplify wrong objectives.
- **Data + hardware + optimization → deep learning:** capability is produced by interacting advances.
- **Capability ≠ benefit:** robust deployment requires safety, transparency, and governance.
