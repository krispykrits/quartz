## Chapter Through-Line
```text
What counts as AI?
      ↓
Human-centered vs rationality-centered views
      ↓
Rational agents → expected utility
      ↓
Limited rationality → computational cost
      ↓
Fixed objectives → alignment / preference uncertainty
      ↓
Foundations from many disciplines
      ↓
History: symbolic AI → expert systems → probabilistic ML → deep learning
      ↓
Capabilities, benefits, risks, governance
```

> [!important]
> The lecture organizes AI around rational action, but then immediately complicates that ideal with uncertainty, computational limits, and imperfectly specified human objectives.
