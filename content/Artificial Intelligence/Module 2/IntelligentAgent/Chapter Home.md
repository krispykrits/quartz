## Chapter Through-Line

**Perceive → choose → act → evaluate → learn.**

The lecture starts with the external definition of an agent, makes "good
behavior" precise through rationality and performance measures,
specifies the design problem with PEAS and environment properties, then
moves inward to agent program structure. It finishes by showing how
agents learn and how their internal world representations range from
atomic to factored to structured.

> [!important] High-Value Study Concept\
> The same agent function can be rational under one task specification
> and irrational under another. Rationality is always relative to the
> performance measure, prior knowledge, available actions, and percept
> sequence.
