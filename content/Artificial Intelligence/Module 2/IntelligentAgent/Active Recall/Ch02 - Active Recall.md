# Chapter 2 — Active Recall

## Easy

1.  What is an agent?
2.  What is the difference between a percept and a percept sequence?
3.  What does the agent function map from and to?
4.  Why must agent function and agent program not be confused?
5.  What does the lecture mean by consequentialism?
6.  What is a performance measure?
7.  Name the four factors on which rationality depends.
8.  Why is rationality not the same as omniscience?
9.  How can information gathering be a rational action?
10. What does autonomy mean for an agent?

## Moderate

11. What does PEAS stand for?
12. Why must PEAS be specified before agent design?
13. Name the seven task-environment dimensions.
14. Why are known/unknown and observable/unobservable independent?
15. What distinguishes stochastic from nondeterministic in this lecture?
16. Why are episodic environments simpler than sequential ones?
17. What is a semidynamic environment?
18. Why does table-driven agent design fail in practice?
19. What information does a simple reflex agent use?
20. What two models are required by a model-based reflex agent?
21. What new capability distinguishes a goal-based agent from a reflex
    agent?
22. Why are goals insufficient when outcomes involve trade-offs or
    uncertainty?

## Difficult

23. State the expected-utility equation and explain it in words.
24. Name the four learning-agent components.
25. What is the critic's role?
26. What is the problem generator's role?
27. What kinds of agent knowledge can the learning element modify?
28. Compare atomic, factored, and structured representations in one
    sentence each.
29. What is the expressiveness trade-off?
30. How is the localist/distributed distinction different from the
    atomic/factored/structured axis?
