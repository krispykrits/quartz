# Spike Time-Dependent Plasticity
*Source: slide 42.*

## Purpose
Change synaptic efficacy according to relative pre/post spike timing.

$$
\Delta T=T_{post}-T_{pre}
$$

The lecture figure plots weight change against this timing difference and labels **LTP** and **LTD** regions.

## Exam-Level Understanding
Know that STDP contains temporal-order information that the basic co-activity Hebb rule does not.

> [!note]
> The lecture slide does not provide a complete analytic STDP update equation, so none is invented here.
