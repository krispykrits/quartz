# Excitation, Inhibition, and Neural Integration
*Source: slides 10–17.*

EPSPs increase membrane potential; IPSPs decrease it. PSPs arriving across dendrites and soma are integrated continuously.

## Dale's Law
The lecture states that a biological presynaptic neuron either excites all targets or inhibits all targets according to neurotransmitter. ANNs typically permit mixed positive and negative outgoing weights.

## Lecture Pattern Example
Slide 16: combined activation exceeds threshold → spike. Slide 17: added inhibition keeps activation below threshold → no spike.
