# Large-Scale Brain Networks
*Source: slides 95–115.*

The cortex is modular across multiple scales. The perception-action cycle is framed as Sense → Think → Act.

Random, small-world, and fat-tailed topologies differ in clustering and degree structure. Functional communities can process separately, while hubs/rich-club structure supports integration.

Structural connectivity is anatomical. Functional connectivity is dynamical/statistical. The lecture introduces transfer entropy for directed functional influence.

Functional communities are dynamic; slide 113 identifies SSM, TIL, TPN, TNN, and VIS and shows major changes across 60-second windows.
