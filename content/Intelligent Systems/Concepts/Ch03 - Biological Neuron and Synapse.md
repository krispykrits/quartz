# Biological Neuron and Synapse
*Source: slides 5–14.*

## Definition
A neuron receives, integrates, and transmits signals. A synapse is a directed junction from a presynaptic to a postsynaptic neuron.

## Technical Explanation
Dendrites and soma receive synaptic input. An incoming presynaptic action potential triggers neurotransmitter release across the synaptic cleft, producing a postsynaptic potential.

Slides 12–14 show presynaptic terminal → synaptic cleft → postsynaptic terminal, with incoming action potential, neurotransmitter release, and generated PSP.
