# Detailed Neural Activation
*Source: slides 75–94.*

Selective permeability and asymmetric ion concentrations generate membrane voltage. Ligand-gated channels respond to chemical binding and contribute to PSPs; voltage-gated channels respond to membrane voltage and generate/propagate action potentials.

Hodgkin-Huxley uses gating variables \(m,h,n\). Sodium conductance is \(g_{Na}m^3h\); potassium conductance is \(g_Kn^4\). Rapid sodium activation depolarizes; later sodium inactivation and potassium activation repolarize/hyperpolarize.

Compartmental models approximate extended neuronal morphology with connected passive and active membrane compartments.
