# Population and Distributed Coding
*Source: slides 44–52.*

Motor-cortex neurons have cosine-like directional tuning; direction is represented across a population. Hippocampal place cells provide location-related activity. Grid cells have periodic grid-like spatial firing fields and are presented as working with place cells. Inferotemporal examples show complex objects represented through combinations of feature-specific activity.

Slides 51–52 show somatosensory and motor homunculi: cortical representation is disproportionate to physical body size.
