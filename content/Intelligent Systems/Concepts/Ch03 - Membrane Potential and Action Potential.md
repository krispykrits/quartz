# Membrane Potential and Action Potential
*Source: slides 7–10, 75–92.*

## Definition
Membrane potential \(V_m\) is voltage across the neuronal membrane. The lecture gives resting potential as roughly -65 to -90 mV. Reaching firing threshold generates an action potential.

## Sequence
PSPs → somatic integration → threshold → action potential → refractory period.

## Saltatory Conduction
Myelin reduces charge leakage; nodes of Ranvier regenerate the action potential.

## Common Confusion
PSPs are graded inputs. The action potential is the regenerative output spike.
