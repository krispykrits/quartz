# Neural Network Architectures
*Source: slides 29–34.*

- CPG: repeating activity for periodic behavior.
- Retinotopic: preserves spatial relationships.
- Lateral inhibition/excitation: structured competition/cooperation.
- Recurrent: feedback supports equilibria, sequences, attractors.
- Feed-forward: lower → higher layers only.
- WTA: lateral inhibition lets an initially advantaged neuron dominate.

## CPG Diagram
Slide 32 shows one unit CPG per limb. Interactions among units create coordinated locomotion under simple top-down drive/modulation.
