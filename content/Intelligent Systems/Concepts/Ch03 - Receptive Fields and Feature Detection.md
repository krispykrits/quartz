# Receptive Fields and Feature Detection
*Source: slides 53–72.*

A receptive field is the sensory region influencing a neuron's response. Retinotopy preserves spatial relationships.

## Center-Surround
On-center/off-surround ganglion cells combine center excitation with surround inhibition, making them responsive to spatial contrast.

## Feature Detection
Visual-cortex neurons can be selective for edges and orientations.

## Letter Recognizer
Feature detectors produce a feature vector. An initial classifier cannot distinguish A and Z; adding inhibition and changing threshold allows the distinction. Sharper detectors excite desired pixels and inhibit the rest.
