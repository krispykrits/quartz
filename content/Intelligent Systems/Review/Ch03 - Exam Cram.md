## If You Only Remember 10 Things
1. Neurons integrate PSPs and fire at threshold.
2. EPSPs excite; IPSPs inhibit.
3. Action potentials propagate regeneratively and are followed by refractoriness.
4. Dale's Law constrains biological output sign; typical ANN weights do not.
5. Information can be encoded in rate, timing, synchrony, and populations.
6. Network architecture changes computation.
7. Basic Hebbian learning is unstable.
8. Gated Hebbian rules have conditional-probability interpretations.
9. Center-surround and feature detectors demonstrate small-network computation.
10. Brain function combines modular segregation with large-scale integration.
