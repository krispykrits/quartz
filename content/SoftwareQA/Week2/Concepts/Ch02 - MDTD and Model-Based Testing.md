# MDTD and Model-Based Testing
**Source:** Section 2.4 sidebar.

Model-based testing (MBT) designs tests from an abstract model representing aspects of software, often behavior.

The chapter says MDTD overlaps strongly with MBT but differs in emphasis:
- MDTD test structures may be created **after implementation** by a tester;
- the structures represent behavior but do not necessarily specify it;
- MDTD often uses idealized mathematical structures more abstract than modeling languages;
- MDTD can derive structures directly from implementation artifacts such as control-flow graphs, call graphs, and decision-condition structures.

## Exam-Level Understanding
MDTD is not simply a synonym for MBT; it is a broader abstraction-centered test-design process in the chapter's framing.
