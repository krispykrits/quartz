# Black-Box and White-Box Testing
**Source:** Section 2.4 sidebar.

Traditionally:
- **Black-box testing** derives tests from external descriptions such as specifications, requirements, and design.
- **White-box testing** derives tests from source-code internals such as branches, conditions, and statements.

The chapter argues that this distinction is often less useful than asking:

> **From what level of abstraction is the structure drawn?**

The same mathematical abstractions can be extracted from source, design, specifications, or requirements.
